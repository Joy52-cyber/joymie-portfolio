IRL Pool Coach: how it works

An AI pool coach I built at Eyeball Games. It reads the table from a photo or the glasses camera and tells you the best shot. Real screens from the app, using a sample table photo.

1

Start

Tap “Analyze a photo” or pick a sample table.

2

Analysing

Phone reads the photo. Top bar: your group (Solids / Stripes).

3

Coach’s pick

Ball, pocket, aim, spin, power. Plus the next shot.

4

Other options

★ = best. Tap another ball. Your balls are circled on the photo.

5

Table map

Top-down view of every ball found.

6

Mic

Tap to talk to the coach. Off by default.

On the Ray-Ban Meta glasses

Say “Coach me”→ Camera reads the table→ Balls stop: it says the best shot→ Repeats every shot→ “Game over” turns the camera off

Everything runs on the phone. No photos or audio leave it.

Prototype. Screens from the phone app; the glasses mode is tested in a browser, not yet on real glasses.
